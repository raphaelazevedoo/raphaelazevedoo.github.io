Case study: a sent message and an incomplete record

The problem

WhatsApp accepted a deal message, but internal finalization failed: the code tried to convert an alphanumeric identifier into a number. The receipt had been saved, while the queue record still did not reflect completion.

The decision

Preserve the identifier in the correct format and check persisted receipts before acting. Recovery of affected records requires confirmation from every destination and no existing completed publication.

The outcome

Records meeting those conditions can be reconciled without resending the confirmed message. When the delivery outcome is uncertain, the workflow requires review before another attempt.

What was verified

  • 26 local WhatsApp bridge tests passed on October 6, 2026.
  • Existing Python tests cover alphanumeric IDs and repeated recovery without duplicate history entries.
  • Implemented revalidation, persistence and individual receipts per destination.

Developed with AI assistance. The Python suite was not rerun during that review because pytest was unavailable. Local tests do not establish delivery to live services. The bridge uses Baileys, an unofficial integration, and its failure handling does not guarantee exactly-once delivery in every scenario.
